{#- JSON-LD helper macros, imported by templates/partials/jsonld.html. ld_source_node renders one schema.org node describing a CITED external article/record, entirely from a source page's front matter (src_type, url, outlet, published). The @type is derived from the hand-authored src_type label so that opinion pieces stay machine-readably labeled as opinion (editorial rule 4) and registry/official records are not misrepresented as press articles. It never emits rating fields: claim statuses on this site describe sourcing, not adjudicated truth (editorial rules 3 & 7) — see scripts/dossier/verify-jsonld.mjs for the build-time guard. -#} {#- `fp` is the citation fingerprint from data/generated/source-fingerprints.json (written by scripts/dossier/build-jsonld-exports.mjs): sha256 over the source's url + retrieved + outlet tuple, recomputable by anyone from the visible fields.
